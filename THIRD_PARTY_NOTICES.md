# Third-party notices

The protocol of this integration (`src/anthem/`) follows the official Anthem
sheet "MRX x40, AVM 70/90 IP & RS-232" v5 (published by Anthem on
anthemav.com, December 2025): commands, queries, answers, error codes and the
per cent volume scale.

The following project was read as an example, nothing of it is installed or
copied: which states answer while the device is in standby (the model and the
power), the input names asked as `IS<n>IN?` after `ICN?` on the x40 models,
and the zones read again after a zone turns on. Its notice is reproduced
below.
